I. — Audio

Audio Engineering

On the
Architecture
of Reverb.

A modular feedback-delay-network reverb, written in C++17. Twenty-one spatial algorithms — from tight studio rooms to galaxy-scale decay — open source, dark UI, deeply modulated.

Open-source modular reverb engine built on Feedback Delay Network (FDN) architecture, offering 21 unique spatial algorithms — from tight studio rooms to galaxy-scale decay. Inspired by Valhalla's VintageVerb and Room.

Written in C++17 with JUCE, targeting VST3 on Windows and macOS. Features deep modulation, integrated 3-band EQ, ducking & gating dynamics, a resizable dark UI, and JSON preset management.

Quantum Research · BA Research

Braid: an
Agentic Framework
for Quantum Intelligence.

Independent research on quantum agentic intelligence systems — predictive simulation, temporal state inference, and cognitive agent architectures modelling entangled computational pathways.

Work conducted under BA Research, including quantum-sim-see-the-future — a quantum-classical hybrid simulator investigating temporal state inference and cognitive agent architectures.
